# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)
**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Company:** Universal Network Development LLC (d/b/a U.N.D Industries)  
**Public Link:** `https://universalnetworkdevelopment.com/dpa.md`  

---

## 1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS
This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs the processing of personal data ("Personal Data") by Universal Network Development LLC ("Company") on behalf of the Customer ("Customer") pursuant to the Master Services Agreement or Terms of Use.

---

## 2. SECURITY CONTROLS & 72-HOUR BREACH NOTIFICATION
Company maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards including TLS 1.3 transport encryption, AES-256 storage encryption, and Argon2id salted password hashing.

**72-Hour Data Breach Notification:** Company will notify Customer within **seventy-two (72) hours** of confirming any security incident affecting Customer Personal Data.

---

## 3. SUBPROCESSOR MANAGEMENT & 30-DAY NOTICE
Company engages authorized subprocessors listed in `https://universalnetworkdevelopment.com/subprocessors.json`. Company will provide Customer at least **thirty (30) days' notice** before onboarding any new subprocessor.

---

## 4. DATA ERASURE & RETENTION
Company deletes or irreversibly anonymizes all Customer Personal Data within **thirty (30) days** of contract termination or verified customer request, except where retention is required by applicable law.

---

## 5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS & EU SCCS
Where Personal Data originating from the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland is transferred to the US, EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) apply automatically.
